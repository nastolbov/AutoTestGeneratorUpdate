NORMALIZATION Plan (Constantine)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE (Problems):
  App, CliMain
    ▼
  MainController, CliRunner  ← DUPLICATION (fan-in:2 each, same logic)
    ▼
  Parser → Generator → Runner → Dao

AFTER (Normalized):
  App, CliMain
    ▼
  GenerationPipeline  ← New coordinator (single source of truth)
    ├─ parse()
    ├─ generate()
    ├─ run()
    └─ report()
    ▼
  Parser ──┐
  Generator├─ (interfaces / composition)
  Runner ──┤
  Dao ─────┘

  TestGenerator →  {OrchestratorWriter}   (delegates)
                    {ScaffoldWriter}       (pom/BaseTest/etc)
                    {PageObjectWriter}     (existing)
                    {TestClassWriter}      (existing)

RESULT:
  ✓ Cohesion in each module: single responsibility
  ✓ Coupling: MainController & CliRunner now thin (just call GenerationPipeline)
  ✓ Data flow clear: AppModel → TestRunResult (no reverse pointers)
  ✓ Leaves (L5) unchanged (high fan-in OK for utilities)
